Our Story

Music Deserves Better

NevPlay was born from a simple frustration: why should listening to your own music feel second-class? We set out to build the player we always wanted — beautiful, fast, private, and entirely offline.

🎯

Our Mission

Give every Android user a world-class local music experience without compromising privacy or requiring subscriptions.

🔭

Our Vision

A future where offline music players are as polished as streaming apps — but owned entirely by the listener.

💚

Our Promise

Free forever, open source, no ads, no tracking. NevPlay will never sell your data because we never collect it.

Journey

How We Got Here

From a weekend prototype to a full-featured premium player — every step driven by listener feedback.

2024

The Idea

A frustration with bloated streaming apps leads to the first NevPlay prototype — a minimal local player with Material Design.

2025

Open Source Launch

NevPlay goes public on GitHub. Synced lyrics, equalizer, and smart playlists join the core playback engine.

2026

The Glass Era

Material You integration, Liquid Glass UI, AI recommendations, and AutoEQ transform NevPlay into a premium experience.
