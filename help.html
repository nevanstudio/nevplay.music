FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Android warn about a "harmful file"?
This is a common false positive for sideloaded APKs. NevPlay is open source — you can verify the code yourself on GitHub. The warning appears because the app isn't distributed through the Play Store.
How do I add synced lyrics?
Place an .lrc file in the same folder as your audio file with a matching filename. NevPlay will automatically detect and sync lyrics during playback. You can also enable automatic lyrics fetching in Settings.
Does NevPlay work with Android Auto?
Yes! NevPlay supports Android Auto with full playback controls, browse-by-artist/album, and voice search. Connect your phone to your car's head unit and NevPlay appears automatically.
How do I use AutoEQ headphone profiles?
Open Settings → Audio → AutoEQ, then search for your headphone model. NevPlay includes thousands of community-tuned profiles that automatically adjust the equalizer for optimal sound on your specific headphones.
Is my listening data collected or shared?
Absolutely not. NevPlay has zero analytics, zero crash reporting, and zero network calls unless you explicitly enable lyrics fetching. All listening stats are stored locally on your device only.
How do I update to the latest version?
Download the latest APK from our download page and install it over your existing version. Your settings, library, and playlists are preserved. Check the updates page for release notes.
