Overview

NevPlay ("we," "our," or "the app") is a local music player for Android developed as free and open-source software. This Privacy Policy explains how NevPlay handles information when you use our application and website.

Our commitment is straightforward: NevPlay does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. All music playback, library management, and listening statistics occur entirely on your device.

Data Collection

NevPlay does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry
  • Music listening history sent to external servers

We do not require account creation, login credentials, or any form of user registration to use NevPlay.

Local Storage

NevPlay stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your music library metadata (titles, artists, albums) scanned from local files
  • Playlists and queue configurations you create
  • App preferences and settings (theme, equalizer profiles, gesture settings)
  • Listening statistics and rhythm charts (stored locally, never transmitted)
  • Downloaded lyrics files cached for offline use

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any server operated by NevPlay. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored NevPlay data.

Network Access

NevPlay is designed to work fully offline. Network access is only used when you explicitly enable optional features:

  • Lyrics fetching: If enabled, NevPlay may request lyrics from third-party providers using song metadata. No personal information is sent — only song title and artist name.
  • App updates: When you visit our website to download updates, standard web server logs may temporarily record your IP address (see Website section below).

All network features can be disabled in Settings, after which NevPlay operates with zero network connectivity.

Third-Party Services

NevPlay is open source and does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Optional lyrics providers operate under their own privacy policies when accessed.

Our website (nevplay.com) uses no cookies, no analytics platforms, and no tracking pixels. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar services.

Children's Privacy

NevPlay does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data from any user, NevPlay is safe for users of all ages.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Material changes will be announced on our updates page. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or NevPlay's data practices, contact us through our GitHub repository issue tracker or community channels linked on our website.
